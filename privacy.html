Your Privacy Commitment

Growing ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our members. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, stored, and protected within the Growing mobile application.

1. Private Net Worth & Financial Tracking

Your itemized assets, liabilities, total net worth calculations, velocity scenarios, and custom target milestone dates are stored strictly on your local device session.

  • Your itemized asset and liability data is never published publicly.
  • Financial figures are confidential to your private user dashboard.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your private financial information.

2. Member Directory & Social Profiles

Growing is an exclusive invite-only community. Information you voluntarily provide during registration or profile updates—such as your First Name, LinkedIn handle, Instagram handle, and motivation statement—is displayed to verified members within the Connect directory to facilitate networking.

3. Personal Passcode & Security

Access to your dashboard is secured by your personal 4-digit passcode. Passcodes are hashed and verified locally. If you reset your passcode, email verification confirms account ownership.

4. WhatsApp Group & Direct Messaging

When you choose to join the official Growing WhatsApp group or click a member's WhatsApp direct link, communication occurs directly via the WhatsApp application subject to WhatsApp's standard privacy practices.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your account data, please submit an inquiry via our Support Center or contact us directly at anelsonp2@gmail.com.
