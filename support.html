Frequently Asked Questions

How do I obtain an invitation code to join Growing? +
Growing is strictly invite-only to maintain a high-trust network of individuals serious about achieving a $1,000,000 net worth goal. Invitation codes are issued directly by the Administrator. If you do not have an invitation code, use the contact form above to request an invite code.
How do I reset my personal 4-digit passcode? +
If you forget your personal 4-digit passcode, tap "Forgot / Reset Passcode?" on the passcode unlock screen in the app. Enter your registered email address to verify ownership and set a new 4-digit passcode immediately.
Is my private financial & net worth data secure? +
Yes. All itemized assets, liabilities, net worth figures, and custom milestone target dates remain 100% private to your local device session. They are never shared publicly or visible to other network members.
How do I join the Growing WhatsApp Group? +
Verified members can tap the "💬 Join Growing WhatsApp Group 🔗" button on the Dashboard tab, Connect tab, or App Instructions Guide to join live member discussions and receive real-time admin announcements.
